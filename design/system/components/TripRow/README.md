# TripRow

A trip on Home, presented editorially: a large photo beside a big title and two or three specific sentences. Rows alternate sides. Use three or four rows on Home and link to the Safaris index for the rest.

- Provide: `images[0]`, `title`, a label line in prose (duration, style if not a standard safari, best months: "Nine days, best June to September"), two or three sentences naming real places and lodges from the itinerary, `price.from` and `price.basis`.
- Write the sentences from the itinerary, not from the old marketing copy. No "breathtaking", no "unforgettable".
- Stacks to one column under 720px.
