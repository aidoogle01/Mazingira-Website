1. Day 3

    Ngorongoro Crater

    After breakfast, drive to the Ngorongoro Crater, a caldera formed about 2.5 million years ago when a huge volcano collapsed. Your driver-guide explains how the Maasai and the wildlife share the conservation area.

    Stay
    Lions Paw Ngorongoro Camp
    Meals
    Breakfast, lunch and dinner
  2. Days 7–8

    Zanzibar

    Two full days on Zanzibar's beaches. Swim, explore Stone Town, or take a boat to Prison Island to see its giant tortoises.

    Stay
    Essque Zalu
    Meals
    Breakfast, lunch and dinner
