# GuideProfile

A page or section for one person: a guide, the founder, a planner. These pages carry more weight than any other content: they are the proof that Mazingira's guides are real, Tanzanian and employed by Mazingira.

- Provide, from `content/team/<id>.json`: a real photo (in uniform, with the vehicle if possible), name, role, two or three facts (where they grew up, speciality, training or years guiding), a short paragraph and, if they said one, a sentence in their own words.
- Write the paragraph in the third person from their bio. Quote only words they actually wrote or said.
- Use the same component for Jacob's founder page, with his background at Asilia (Sokwe), Nomad Tanzania, Wayo Africa and Kibo Guides as the facts.
- Show "Your guide" with a link to the profile on trip pages once guides are assigned.
