Safari guide

Goodluck Nathan

Grew up
On the slopes of Mount Meru, Arusha region
Speciality
Birds: identification, habits and habitats
Trained
Diploma in tourism and hospitality (2021); a year as a game-package guide on the Mara River

Goodluck is one of the youngest guides from the Meru community. He grew up with an uncle who was a tour guide and conservationist, spent four years studying birds before guiding college, and started as a transfer guide around Arusha and Kilimanjaro before joining Mazingira full-time.

"I thoroughly enjoy nature, particularly birding."
