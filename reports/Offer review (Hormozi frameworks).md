# Offer review: Alex Hormozi's frameworks applied to the Mazingira site

Applied on 1 November 2026 to Home, the Safaris index, trip pages and the enquiry block. The frameworks come from *$100M Offers* (the value equation, naming, risk reversal) and *$100M Leads* (a clear call to action, proof next to it). They are adapted to the brand rules in `design/system/README.md`: no hype, no invented guarantees, scarcity or bonuses. Anything a framework needs that Mazingira hasn't confirmed has become a question in `content/_review/client-answers.json`, not copy.

## The value equation

Value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood of getting it) ÷ (time delay × effort and sacrifice).

| Lever | What the site did before | What it does now | Still needs Mazingira |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Trip names were vague ("Serengeti Escape A Romantic Adventure") | Names say who the trip is for and where it goes ("Serengeti for two", "Great Migration river crossings"); photo bands show the outcome | Approve the names (C-titles) |
| Perceived likelihood | One review score, in the hero only | Both scores (Tripadvisor 5.0 from 120, SafariBookings 5.0 from 81) beside the hero buttons and on the Safaris index; own guides, since 2007, TATO | Confirm the counts (Q14) |
| Time delay | Nothing said about how fast Johari replies | Unchanged on the page: the enquiry block shows `site.reply_promise` only once it exists | A reply time they can keep every day (Q21) |
| Effort and sacrifice | Calls to action asked for work ("Plan your safari", "Send enquiry") | Calls to action name what you get ("Get your itinerary and price", "Send to Johari"); "Every safari price includes" lists the private 4x4, own guide, park fees, transfers, meals and lodges | |

## Risk reversal

Hormozi's strongest lever is a guarantee. Mazingira has none on record, so the site states only what the booking terms already promise: you pay nothing until you've approved a written quote, then a 30% deposit. It appears on Home, beside the enquiry button and in each trip's price section. Q22 offers three guarantee options for Mazingira to accept or decline.

## Scarcity and urgency

Not used. July to September and the river-crossing camps may genuinely book up early, but that is unconfirmed (Q23). The best-months strip already gives an honest reason to decide.

## Not done, and why

- **Bonuses** (free extras to make the offer bigger): none are invented. The sunset picnic add-on (Q10e) could become a honeymoon bonus if Mazingira wants that.
- **A lead magnet** (*$100M Leads*): a free month-by-month Tanzania planner, exchanged for an email address, would capture people who aren't ready to enquire. It needs the form service and a newsletter tool first.
- **Countdown timers, "only 2 left" or all-caps urgency**: excluded by the brand rules and the research (`reports/Safari website benchmarks and AI design.md`): this buyer is spending $2,000–$9,000 a head and reads pressure as a warning sign.
